# Microwave oven: heat is random collisions, so jostle the molecules with an electric field flipped faster until they cannot keep up

**Chain at a glance:** Shake a box by hand -> Vibrating plate under container -> Flip electricity by hand -> Motor flips the electricity -> Circuit, thousands of flips/second -> Millions of flips per second -> Billions of flips per second

**The simple problem:** Heat water without fire; shaking a box of beads, or of water, heats it through collisions. [00:00]

## The ladder

### 1. Shake the box [00:00]
- **Idea:** Shake a box of beads, then of water, like crazy.
- **Why it works:** Colliding particles turn motion into heat.
- **But:** You cannot shake it every time you want hot water. [00:14] - `manual-effort`
- **So:** A vibrating plate under the container. [00:21] - `automate`
- **Shown as:** The engineer shakes a clear box of steel beads; the water box turns red as it heats.
![Water box glowing red](frames/microwave-oven/f003_0-00-11.jpg)

### 2. Vibrating plate [00:21]
- **Idea:** A machine shakes only the container.
- **Why it works:** The water does move.
- **But:** All molecules move the same way, never collide; no heat. [00:24] - `doesn't-work`
- **So:** Molecules respond to electricity, so reverse an electric direction instead of moving a plate. [00:37] - `field-not-motion`
- **Shown as:** Ripples in a glass of water that stays clear, then glassy molecule balls drifting together.
![Ripples but no heat](frames/microwave-oven/f007_0-00-26.jpg)

### 3. Flip the electricity by hand [00:41]
- **Idea:** Reverse the electric direction across the water.
- **Why it works:** Each molecule has + and - ends, so it turns to follow.
- **But:** They turn too neatly together. [00:43] - `too-weak-or-slow`
- **So:** Flip faster with a motor. [00:48] - `push-parameter`
- **Shown as:** A +/- labelled molecule swings; the engineer works a switch above a grid of molecules all facing one way.
![Switch and orderly molecules](frames/microwave-oven/f012_0-00-45.jpg)

### 4. Motor-driven flipping [00:48]
- **Idea:** A motor reverses the direction.
- **Why it works:** More flips per second.
- **But:** Still too gentle to create heat. - `too-weak-or-slow`
- **So:** A circuit flipping thousands of times per second. [00:52] - `push-parameter`
- **Shown as:** Close-up of a small motor on the desk.
![Motor](frames/microwave-oven/f013_0-00-49.jpg)

### 5. Circuit, thousands per second [00:52]
- **Idea:** Electronic instead of mechanical switching.
- **Why it works:** A circuit outpaces any motor.
- **But:** The molecules still move in line. [00:57] - `too-weak-or-slow`
- **So:** Millions of times per second. [00:59] - `push-parameter`
- **Shown as:** Circuit board and oscilloscope above a still-orderly molecule grid.
![Oscilloscope over orderly molecules](frames/microwave-oven/f015_0-00-58.jpg)

### 6. Millions per second [00:59]
- **Idea:** Raise the rate again.
- **Why it works:** Molecules start lagging behind.
- **But:** They only slowly begin to collide. [01:02] - `too-weak-or-slow`
- **So:** "The problem was speed": billions per second. [01:05] - `push-parameter`
- **Shown as:** The oscilloscope wave packs denser.
![Denser waveform](frames/microwave-oven/f016_0-01-01.jpg)

### Final: Billions per second, the microwave oven [01:05]
- **Idea:** Flip the field billions of times per second.
- **Why it solves the chain:** Molecules cannot keep up and crash randomly [01:09]: the shaken box's collisions with no shaking [01:16], from a circuit alone [01:18].
- **Reveal:** After the box callback, "This is the Microwave Oven" [01:20] as an oven appears on the desk.
![Microwave oven on the desk](frames/microwave-oven/f021_0-01-22.jpg)

## Accuracy notes
- Real mechanism is dielectric loss: polar molecules try to rotate with the field, lag and lose energy to neighbours; "crashing" is a cartoon, and absorption rises gradually, not at a threshold. Ovens use about 2.45 GHz, below water's peak, so waves penetrate food.
- History differs: around 1945 Percy Spencer noticed a radar magnetron melting a candy bar; the source is a magnetron tube, not an ordinary circuit.
- Strong vibration (ultrasound) does warm water slightly.
